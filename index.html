01 / About

Curiosity, made practical.

I like going beyond using technology to understanding the systems and ideas behind it.

Computer science is more than a subject to me; it is a way to explore, reason, and create. I am especially interested in how a problem can be broken down, modelled clearly, and solved with a program that is useful in the real world.

My interest in programming began early and has grown into a broader fascination with computing: the logic behind software, the tools developers use, and the hardware that makes it all possible. I enjoy learning by building, testing my assumptions, and improving a project until the details make sense.

I also enjoy making things outside the screen. Exploring 3D printing and 3D modelling gives me a tangible connection between digital design and the physical world. I want to keep developing the skills to move comfortably between software, systems, and hands-on experimentation.
